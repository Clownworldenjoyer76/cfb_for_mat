Key minimal-change principle

The place to absorb CFB differences is at the raw intake boundary, not throughout the pipeline.

For example:

ESPN CFB PBP → normalize to existing NFL PBP columns → reuse pull_team_stats.py

rather than rewriting pull_team_stats.py and everything downstream.

Likewise:

ESPN CFB depth chart → reshape to existing raw_depth.csv format → reuse depth_cleanup.py.

One other point from inspecting the folder: there is no team_master.py in your current 00_intake scripts, so I am not inventing one. team_master.csv and its supporting mappings are foundation files that need to be established before scripts that consume them.
