## Prediction Enrichment ##
docs/win/football/nfl/config/prediction_enrichment/moneyline_enrichment.csv is a machine-readable translation of the actionable findings from the historical ALL4 test.

Critical fact: every row measures whether a selected team won the game outright. SpreadBucket and OddsTotalBucket are only conditions describing the game; they are not ATS or over/under results. The historical script explicitly created one selection row per selected side and set Won by comparing that side to the actual game winner. Pasted text.txt

What one row means

Example conceptually:

EPRED | SpreadBucket=Fav -10+ | historical_win_rate=88.37%

means:

When EPRED selected a team, and that selected team was a market favorite of -10 or greater, the EPRED-selected team won the game outright 88.37% of the time.

It does NOT mean the favorite covered -10 88.37% of the time.

The 25 unsupported rows

Those rules were retained but marked inactive/unsupported because the current pipeline cannot reproduce the historical condition correctly:

Historical feature	Reason

Most important interpretation

A rule such as:

SpreadBucket=Dog +0.5 to +2.5

means:

How often did the selected team win the game outright when it happened to be a +0.5 to +2.5 underdog?

A rule such as:

OddsTotalBucket=48-51.5

means:

How often did the selected team win outright when the game’s market total was 48–51.5?

Neither field measures covering a spread or going over/under. That distinction comes directly from how the historical selection rows and Won outcome were constructed. Pasted text.txt

docs/win/football/nfl/config/prediction_enrichment/spread_enrichment.csv is a machine-readable translation of the actionable findings from the historical ALL4 ATS test.

Critical fact

Every row measures whether the team selected by that prediction family covered its spread.

The historical ATS script created one selection row per family-selected side and calculated:

ATS margin = selected team score − opponent score + selected team spread

Then:

* ATS margin > 0 = Cover
* ATS margin < 0 = ATS Loss
* ATS margin = 0 = Push

Pushes were excluded from historical cover-rate calculations.

OddsTotalBucket, WeekBucket, RatingGapBucket, and the other feature buckets are only conditions describing the game or selected team. They are not separate prediction targets.

SpreadBucket is slightly different because the selected team’s spread is both:

1. Part of the ATS outcome calculation, and
2. A possible historical condition describing the magnitude of that spread.

⸻

What one row means

Example conceptually:

DRAT | WeekBucket=Weeks 1-3 | ProbBucket=60-64.9% | historical_cover_rate=32.79%

means:

When DRAT selected a team during Weeks 1–3, and DRAT assigned that selected team a 60–64.9% win probability, the DRAT-selected team covered its market spread 32.79% of the time historically.

It does not mean DRAT won outright 32.79% of the time.

It does not mean games went Over or Under at that rate.

The target is specifically whether the family-selected team covered ATS.

docs/win/football/nfl/config/prediction_enrichment/totals_enrichment.csv is a machine-readable translation of the actionable findings from the corrected historical ALL4 totals test.

Critical fact

Every row measures whether a specific Over or Under direction hit.

The prediction family does not itself select Over or Under.

Instead, DRAT, EPRED, MARKET, DRAT_EPRED_CONSENSUS, or ALL3_CONSENSUS selects a team. That selected team establishes the pregame context used to calculate features such as:

* selected probability
* selected-team spread
* market role
* EPRED rating gap
* model disagreement
* model-vs-market edge

The historical totals test then evaluated that same family/game context twice:

1. Once as Over
2. Once as Under

The totals outcome was determined only by comparing the final combined score with the market total.

Exact-total pushes were excluded.

⸻

What one row means

Example:

ALL3_CONSENSUS | Under | WeekBucket=Weeks 1-3 | ProbBucket=60-64.9% | historical_hit_rate=66.13%

means:

When DRAT, EPRED, and market all selected the same team, their consensus probability for that selected team was 60–64.9%, and the game occurred in Weeks 1–3, the Under hit 66.13% of the time historically.

It does not mean the consensus-selected team won 66.13% of the time.

It does not mean the consensus-selected team covered 66.13% of the time.

It means the game finished Under the market total 66.13% of the time under that family-defined game context.

⸻

totals_direction

This column is unique to the totals master.

Value	Meaning
Over	Historical target was whether final combined points finished above the market total.
Under	Historical target was whether final combined points finished below the market total.

For every eligible family/game:

ActualTotal = home final score + away final score

For an Over row:

TotalsMargin = ActualTotal − MarketTotal

For an Under row:

TotalsMargin = MarketTotal − ActualTotal

Then:

* Margin > 0 = Win/Hit
* Margin < 0 = Loss
* Actual total exactly equals market total = Push

Pushes are excluded from hit-rate calculations.

Baseline interpretation

Totals uses a separate baseline for:

* each family, and
* each totals direction.

For example:

DRAT Over

and

DRAT Under

have separate historical baselines.

The historical test therefore does not simply compare every totals condition against 50%.

A rule’s lift is measured relative to the normal historical performance of that exact family/direction combination.
