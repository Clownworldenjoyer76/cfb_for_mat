docs/win/football/nfl/config/line_movement.csv

This file is the normalized historical NFL line-movement and betting-market lookup table used to compare current NFL odds conditions with historical results.

Each row represents either a specific historical betting condition or a broader historical baseline. The file covers spread movement, total movement, opening-line ranges, exact movement amounts, movement-direction buckets, and combined closing spread/total conditions.

Column definitions

Column	Meaning
rule_id	Unique shorthand identifier for the historical condition. The ID encodes the market and type of rule so it can be referenced consistently by the pipeline.
market_type	Market represented by the row. Values include spread, total, and joint. joint means the rule uses both the closing spread and closing total.
analysis_family	Identifies what kind of historical comparison the row represents, such as exact movement, opening-line bucket plus movement, opening-line baseline, movement baseline, or closing spread combined with closing total.
row_type	rule for a specific historical condition or baseline for a broader comparison group.
opening_bucket	Range containing the opening spread or opening total. Examples: 3-6.5, 10-13.5, 36-40.5, 46-50.5, 51+. Blank when the rule does not depend on the opening line.
movement	Amount or category of movement from the opening line. It may be an exact number such as -2.5 or a bucket such as 2-2.5 toward underdog, 0.5-1.5 toward over, or same.
closing_spread_bucket	Closing spread range used by combined spread/total rules. Example: 7-9.5.
closing_total_bucket	Closing total range used by combined spread/total rules. Example: 46-50.5.
reference_side	The side whose historical performance is recorded in wins, losses, and rate_pct. Examples: opening_favorite, favorite, or over.
outcome_type	What constitutes a historical win for the row. cover means ATS performance. over means the game finished Over the total.
games	Number of historical games matching the condition.
wins	Historical wins for the reference_side.
losses	Historical losses for the reference_side.
pushes	Historical pushes for the condition.
rate_pct	Historical success percentage for the reference_side, excluding pushes from the win-rate denominator where applicable.
quality_flag	Classification attached to the row. Common values are ok and small_sample, with other flags available for rows that were specially identified during normalization.

How to read rule_id

The ID is shorthand for the condition represented by the row.

Market prefixes

Prefix	Meaning
SP	Spread
TOT	Game total / Over-Under
JT	Joint closing spread + closing total condition

⸻

Spread rule IDs

SP_MV_EX_-11.5

Breakdown:

* SP = spread
* MV = movement
* EX = exact movement
* -11.5 = exact movement amount

This row means:

Historically, when the NFL spread moved exactly 11.5 points toward the opening favorite, how often did the opening favorite cover?

For exact spread-movement rules:

* Negative movement = movement toward the opening favorite
* Positive movement = movement toward the opening underdog
* 0 = closing spread was the same as the opening spread

Example:

SP_MV_EX_-2

means an exact 2-point move toward the opening favorite.

SP_MV_EX_3.5

means an exact 3.5-point move toward the opening underdog.

The historical result is always measured from the perspective of reference_side = opening_favorite.

⸻

SP_OB_10_13.5_M2D

Breakdown:

* SP = spread
* OB = opening spread bucket
* 10_13.5 = opening favorite was between 10 and 13.5 points
* M2D = movement of 2 to 2.5 points toward the opening underdog

This means:

The opening favorite was favored by 10 to 13.5 points, and the line subsequently moved 2 to 2.5 points toward the opening underdog.

For this example the historical result is:

* 32 games
* Opening favorite: 19-13 ATS
* Cover rate: 59.4%

Spread movement bucket codes

Code	Meaning
M3F	3+ points toward opening favorite
M2F	2-2.5 points toward opening favorite
M1F	0.5-1.5 points toward opening favorite
M0	No movement
M1D	0.5-1.5 points toward opening underdog
M2D	2-2.5 points toward opening underdog
M3D	3+ points toward opening underdog

F therefore means favorite and D means underdog.

⸻

SP_OB_BASE_10_13.5

Breakdown:

* SP = spread
* OB_BASE = opening spread bucket baseline
* 10_13.5 = opening favorite of 10 through 13.5

This is not tied to one movement category.

It represents the historical ATS performance of opening favorites in that opening spread range across the full bucket.

Example:

SP_OB_BASE_10_13.5

means:

How often did opening favorites of 10 to 13.5 points cover overall?

This baseline can be compared with more specific movement conditions within the same opening range.

⸻

SP_MB_2D

Breakdown:

* SP = spread
* MB = movement bucket baseline
* 2D = 2 to 2.5 points toward the opening underdog

This represents:

How did opening favorites perform ATS overall whenever the spread moved 2 to 2.5 points toward the opening underdog, regardless of the opening spread range?

These rows provide broad movement-history baselines.

⸻

Total rule IDs

TOT_MV_EX_-2

Breakdown:

* TOT = game total
* MV = movement
* EX = exact movement
* -2 = total moved exactly 2 points downward

For exact total-movement rules:

* Positive movement = total moved toward the Over / upward
* Negative movement = total moved toward the Under / downward
* 0 = closing total was the same as the opening total

Therefore:

TOT_MV_EX_2.5

means the total increased exactly 2.5 points.

TOT_MV_EX_-2.5

means the total decreased exactly 2.5 points.

The historical result is measured using:

reference_side = over

so rate_pct represents the historical Over percentage.

⸻

TOT_OB_46_50.5_M2U

Breakdown:

* TOT = total
* OB = opening total bucket
* 46_50.5 = opening total between 46 and 50.5
* M2U = movement of 2 to 2.5 points toward the Under

This means:

The game opened with a total between 46 and 50.5, and the total subsequently moved 2 to 2.5 points downward toward the Under.

The historical wins/losses are recorded from the Over perspective.

For example:

TOT_OB_46_50.5_M2U

contains:

* 163 games
* Over record: 87-72-4
* Over rate: 54.7%

So even though the market movement was toward the Under, rate_pct tells you how frequently the Over historically won in that exact condition.

Total movement bucket codes

Code	Meaning
M3O	3+ points toward Over
M2O	2-2.5 points toward Over
M1O	0.5-1.5 points toward Over
M0	No movement
M1U	0.5-1.5 points toward Under
M2U	2-2.5 points toward Under
M3U	3+ points toward Under

O means Over and U means Under.

⸻

TOT_OB_BASE_46_50.5

This is the broader historical baseline for games whose opening total was between 46 and 50.5.

It answers:

Across all games opening from 46 through 50.5, how often did the game go Over?

It can be compared with a specific rule such as:

TOT_OB_46_50.5_M2U

to see how the movement condition performed relative to the normal historical behavior of that opening-total range.

⸻

TOT_MB_3U

Breakdown:

* TOT = total
* MB = movement bucket baseline
* 3U = total moved 3+ points toward the Under

This represents:

Across all opening totals, how often did games go Over when the total moved at least 3 points toward the Under?

It is a broad movement baseline rather than an opening-line-specific condition.

⸻

Joint spread + total rules

JT_7_9.5_46_50.5_FAV

Breakdown:

* JT = joint spread/total rule
* 7_9.5 = closing favorite spread between 7 and 9.5
* 46_50.5 = closing total between 46 and 50.5
* FAV = historical favorite ATS result

This means:

When the closing favorite was laying between 7 and 9.5 points and the closing game total was between 46 and 50.5, how often did the favorite cover?

The corresponding row:

JT_7_9.5_46_50.5_OVER

uses exactly the same closing-line condition but measures how often the game went Over.

Therefore the pair:

* JT_7_9.5_46_50.5_FAV
* JT_7_9.5_46_50.5_OVER

describes two different historical outcomes for the same market setup.

⸻

Meaning of analysis_family

movement_exact

Uses one exact amount of line movement.

Examples:

* spread moved exactly 2 points
* total moved exactly -1.5 points

Rule IDs generally contain:

MV_EX

⸻

opening_bucket_x_movement

Combines:

1. the opening spread or opening total range, and
2. the subsequent movement direction/magnitude.

Examples:

* opening favorite 10-13.5 + move 2-2.5 toward underdog
* opening total 46-50.5 + move 2-2.5 toward Under

These are the SP_OB_* and TOT_OB_* rules.

⸻

opening_bucket_baseline

Historical performance for an opening-line range without restricting it to one movement category.

Examples:

SP_OB_BASE_3_6.5

TOT_OB_BASE_46_50.5

⸻

movement_bucket_baseline

Historical performance for one general movement category regardless of opening-line range.

Examples:

SP_MB_3F

TOT_MB_2U

⸻

closing_spread_x_total

Uses the closing point-spread range and closing total range together.

These are the JT_* rules.

⸻

Practical use

A current NFL game can be converted into the same conditions represented in this file.

For example, suppose a game:

* opened with the favorite at -11
* current/closing favorite is -8.5
* therefore moved 2.5 points toward the opening underdog

The game matches:

SP_OB_10_13.5_M2D

because:

* opening favorite falls in 10-13.5
* movement is 2-2.5 toward underdog

The pipeline can then attach that rule’s historical:

* sample size
* ATS record
* cover percentage
* rule ID

Likewise, if a game:

* opened at total 48.5
* moved to 46.5

it matches:

TOT_OB_46_50.5_M2U

because:

* opening total was in 46-50.5
* total moved exactly 2 points toward the Under

The file therefore provides a consistent lookup layer that turns raw NFL opening/current market data into identifiable historical betting conditions and their associated historical performance.
